# AI Governance: Risk Assessment Matrix

This sample AI risk assessment matrix provides a framework for evaluating and managing risks associated with AI systems, aligned with ISO 42001 principles.

## Risk Assessment Matrix (Sample)

| Risk Category | Potential Impact | Probability | Mitigation Strategy | ISO 42001 Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bias & Fairness** | Reputational damage, legal action, unfair outcomes. | Medium | Regular bias testing, diverse training data, human oversight. | 8.2.2 (Data for AI) |
| **Transparency & Explainability** | Lack of trust, difficulty in troubleshooting. | Low | Use of explainable AI (XAI) techniques, detailed model documentation. | 8.3.1 (Information for AI stakeholders) |
| **Security & Privacy** | Data breaches, adversarial attacks on models. | Medium | Data encryption, model robustness testing, strict access controls. | 8.2.4 (IT Security) |
| **Safety & Performance** | Operational disruptions, potential physical harm. | Low | Rigorous testing in sandbox environments, fail-safe mechanisms. | 8.2.5 (Safety for AI systems) |
| **Regulatory Compliance** | Legal penalties, inability to operate. | High | Ongoing monitoring of AI regulations, regular compliance audits. | 9.3 (Compliance) |

### Impact/Probability Legend:
*   **Low:** Minor impact/Infrequent occurrence.
*   **Medium:** Moderate impact/Possible occurrence.
*   **High:** Significant impact/Frequent occurrence.
