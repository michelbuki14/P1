# AI Governance: Lifecycle Oversight Outline

This document describes the AI system lifecycle and governance controls at each stage, aligned with ISO 42001 requirements.

## AI System Lifecycle Phases

1.  **Conception & Requirements**
    *   Define the AI system's purpose and scope.
    *   Identify potential ethical and legal risks.
    *   Establish success metrics and performance benchmarks.

2.  **Design & Development**
    *   Select appropriate AI models and training data.
    *   Implement data governance practices (data quality, bias assessment).
    *   Document architectural decisions and model development processes.

3.  **Deployment & Operationalization**
    *   Conduct rigorous testing and validation in a staging environment.
    *   Establish monitoring systems for model performance, drift, and security.
    *   Implement access controls and incident response protocols.

4.  **Monitoring & Management**
    *   Ongoing monitoring of the AI system's performance and impact.
    *   Regularly assess the model's fairness and accuracy.
    *   Perform periodic audits of the AI system and its governance controls.

5.  **Retirement & Disposal**
    *   Establish criteria for retiring an AI system.
    *   Securely decommission the system and dispose of associated data.
    *   Document the lessons learned from the system's lifecycle.

## Governance Controls

*   **Human Oversight:** Ensure human involvement in critical decisions.
*   **Documentation:** Maintain comprehensive records throughout the AI lifecycle.
*   **Transparency:** Provide clear information to stakeholders about the AI system.
*   **Accountability:** Assign clear roles and responsibilities for AI governance.
*   **Continuous Improvement:** Regularly review and update AI governance processes.
